How this is worked out

Your solar is treated as surplus: power that would otherwise go unused, so every sat mined is extra. Every day the network finds 144 blocks. Your share of each block is your average hashrate that day divided by the network's, times the subsidy for that block height plus your fee estimate, less pool fee and boot and thermal losses. Braiins Pool pays on FPPS, so you're paid this expected share every day whether or not the pool finds a block. The subsidy follows the protocol exactly: 5,000,000,000 sats halved every 210,000 blocks and rounded down, reaching 1 sat in the last period and zero from block 6,930,000.

Your average hashrate comes from the solar model. Each month's energy is spread over an average day shaped like a sine curve, with day length from your latitude. With the sunny/cloudy mix on, 60% of days get 1.3× the average and 40% get 0.55×. In on/off mode the miner runs only when solar covers its rated power. When underclocking, it follows the sun between its lowest setting and full power, with hashrate assumed to scale with power to the 0.75.

Network hashrate grows smoothly at the rate you set, and fees per block stay constant in sats. The current block height is estimated from a known recent height at 10 minutes a block unless you enter it. The solar yield presets are rough typical values; use Custom with a PVGIS estimate for your own roof.

An estimate, not a promise: network hashrate, fees and weather will differ from any single assumption. Inputs are saved in this browser only.
